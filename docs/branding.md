# Branding

Countora's editable source mark lives at `assets/branding/countora-mark.svg`.

## Concept

The white open countdown ring forms a visual **C** for Countora. The yellow hand communicates active timing without relying on text. The rounded tile works as a starting point for launcher icons, store artwork, documentation, and splash treatments.

## Usage

- Keep clear space around the mark equal to at least 12% of its width.
- Do not stretch or skew the artwork.
- Preserve sufficient contrast when placing it on another background.
- Derive raster platform assets from this SVG in the release toolchain; do not repeatedly edit generated PNGs by hand.
- Keep the source artwork in version control so future icon/splash changes remain reviewable.
